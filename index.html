Welcome to The Coffee Shop

Most local coffee shops just sell caffeine, but we sell a true neighborhood break. The Coffee Shop is a warm, plant-filled cafe that pairs ethically sourced, single-origin espresso with house-made pastries. We provide remote workers with fast Wi-Fi and high-end craft coffee, transforming a daily errand into a relaxing community ritual.

Whether you are looking to fuel a productive morning of remote work, meet up with a close friend, or simply pause and enjoy a quiet moment with a perfectly poured latte, you will find a welcoming seat waiting for you here. We slow down the brewing process so you can slow down your day.
