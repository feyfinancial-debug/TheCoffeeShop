Our Services and Features

  • Espresso: A strong, concentrated shot of pure coffee served in a small cup.
  • Americano: A shot of espresso diluted with hot water, creating a lighter coffee.
  • Latte: A creamy coffee drink made with espresso and steamed milk, often topped with foam.
  • Cappuccino: Similar to a latte, but with more foam and a stronger coffee flavor.
  • Macchiato: A shot of espresso with a small amount of steamed milk and foam.
  • Fresh pastries
  • Free Wi-Fi for remote workers
  • Comfortable seating area
  • Specialty coffee selections
